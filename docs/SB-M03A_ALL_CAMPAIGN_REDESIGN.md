## Product strategy
Build · Transform · Explore · Invent. Adventure first: discover a problem, explore routes, choose tools, act or code, observe world feedback, finish/resume, unlock new capabilities. Coding is a purposeful tool, not identical required steps in every level. Each existing ID keeps its mapping to legacy name, learning goal and saved progress. No forced quizzes, timers, streak pressure or new tracking. Distinct primary interaction and objective for every ID, not merely renamed maps or longer tracks. Reuse accepted mechanics with meaningful variation. All 24 design records are in src/game/adventureMissionDesign.ts, including difficulty (1–5), target distinct areas and target session minutes; not measured or implemented duration. Future 100-level expansion only after all existing redesigns pass.

## Acceptance per mission
1. Actual primary mechanic and meaningful decision differ from adjacent missions; verify successful and invalid paths, retry/recovery, deterministic action replay. Route and area counts are authored and audited, not arbitrary longer MOVE sequences.
2. First goal understandable via icon/audio without mandatory reading. Physical touch/keyboard, portrait and landscape, no overlap, clear exit, optional voice. Home redesign in separate reconciled #42 follow-up: character unobscured, one primary adventure CTA, Daily and Games secondary.
3. Observe child chooses and understands route and goal, note independent/hint/demonstration/not attempted, replay preference, session duration only voluntarily; no efficacy claim. Record every ID individually, not a blanket PASS.
4. Existing 24 IDs, 7 games, local saves and offline behavior remain; future migration explicit, idempotent and tested. No production, native or commercial release. Rollback by abandoning isolated branch; stable archive and existing main/preview unchanged.
