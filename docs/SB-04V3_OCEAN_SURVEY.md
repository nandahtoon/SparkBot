# SB-04V3 — Ocean environment and survey interaction
Reef Survey (practice 6103) introduces two distinct observation stations along a floating walkway. WAIT on each marked normal tile records a simulated observation; duplicate/wrong-tile waits do not count. Arrival without both observations fails. No water physics, marine measurement data, or free navigation claimed. Uses existing movement/WAIT controls, route-only victory, no campaign reward/save writes. Visual prototype includes a water surface, sand islands, coral-inspired silhouettes, surface ripples, and survey deck markers. Ocean theme selected only for this prototype; other scenes retain existing towers and windmills. Shared/instanced background meshes and tracked disposal; actual rendering quality, mobile FPS, readability and touch require physical device QA. Four behavior tests cover progress, invalid goals, scene selection and legacy separation. Chapter 6 missions 21–24 remain unimplemented; stable main untouched.
