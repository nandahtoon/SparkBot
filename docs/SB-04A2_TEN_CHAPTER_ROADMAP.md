# SparkBot — Play & Learn | Ten-chapter campaign roadmap

## Target
40 total campaign missions, 10 chapters. Preserve all 16 existing IDs, saves, access rules, art and mechanics. Four new levels per chapter (5–10). New chapters ship incrementally, not as empty playable placeholders. Age bands are guidance, not restrictions.

## Quality gates per chapter
- Distinct tracks and learning goals; no duplicated level renamed as new content.
- Deterministic budget-valid route and optical solution; initially unsolved optical challenge; validate star/par targets, hints and actual UI block limits.
- Existing save IDs and progress preserved; campaign map/home/next, victory, retry, pause and replay verified.
- New biome visual targets require independent mobile graphics, performance and accessibility QA. Chapter 5 reuses existing scene art and optics patterns intentionally; no claim of new world geometry.
- Learning goal, optional reflection, and transfer activity; do not treat completion as mastery.
- 44px touch targets, non-color cues, no compulsory timer, no added child data or network collection.
- Chapter 5 first playable increment; 6–10 are proposed work packages, not implemented. Physical device QA and release integration pending.
